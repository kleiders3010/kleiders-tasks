# Play listing (en-US)

**Title (30):** Tasks: Shared Lists

**Short description (80):** To-do and shopping lists you can share. No account, no ads, open source.

**Full description:**

Tasks is a simple list app for to-dos and shopping, made to be quick: open it and type.

• Opens on your last list, ready to add to. Press Enter and keep typing.
• Write "tomorrow at 5" or "friday" and it becomes a reminder.
• Shopping lists: ticked items wait in "Completed", and typing one brings it back with a tap.
• Share any list with a link, a QR code or an 8-word code. Everyone sees changes as they happen.
• Use it on your phone and your Windows computer.
• Steps inside tasks, notes, pictures, and a home screen widget.
• Swipe to delete, with Undo.

No account, no sign-up, no ads, nothing to set up.
Your lists live on your devices. When you share, changes travel end-to-end encrypted between your devices, directly or through public relays and our small mailbox, none of which can read them. Nothing readable is stored on any server, and the app keeps working without ours.

Open source: github.com/kleiders3010/kleiders-tasks
